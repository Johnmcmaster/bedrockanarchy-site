How anonymity works here. No account, no email, no username, no cookie, no analytics, and no request to any third party — this page loads nothing from outside this domain, so no font CDN or tracker ever sees your IP.

What we cannot promise. Your connection still reaches a server, and that server sits on somebody's network. If you need protection from an adversary who can watch traffic, use Tor. No website can do that part for you, and any site that claims otherwise is lying to you.

Sealed posts. Any post can be encrypted in your browser with a passphrase before it is sent. The server stores only ciphertext and never sees the passphrase. Use it for coordinates.
